About Converted

Converted is a free unit converter for everyday and study use. It covers length, weight, temperature, volume, speed and area, and gives you the result instantly as you type, with no sign-up.

How it works

The conversions run in your browser, so the numbers you enter are never sent to a server. Results use the standard international unit definitions, for example 1 inch = 2.54 centimetres and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kilograms.

Who made it

Converted is built by Ehan, a software engineering student, using Python (Flask) and JavaScript. It is a one-person project, and new units and categories are added over time.
