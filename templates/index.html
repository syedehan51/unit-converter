Quick answers

How many kilograms are in a pound?

One pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms, so multiply pounds by 0.4536 for a quick estimate.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 (1.8), then add 32. For example, 20°C is 20 × 1.8 + 32 = 68°F.
